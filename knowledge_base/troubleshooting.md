# Bearing Troubleshooting Guide

## Symptom-Based Diagnosis

### High Vibration — Overall Level Elevated

**Possible causes:**
1. Bearing wear or damage (most common)
2. Misalignment (shaft or coupling)
3. Imbalance in rotating parts
4. Looseness (bearing housing, foundation bolts)
5. Resonance condition
6. Overloading

**Diagnostic steps:**
1. Check vibration spectrum for frequency pattern
2. If bearing frequencies present → bearing damage likely
3. Check alignment of shaft and coupling
4. Check for structural looseness
5. Verify load conditions are within specification

### High Kurtosis / Crest Factor

**Possible causes:**
1. Early-stage bearing fault (impulsive impacts)
2. Contamination in bearing
3. Surface fatigue (pitting, spalling)

**Diagnostic steps:**
1. Kurtosis > 4.0 → inspect bearing soon
2. Kurtosis > 6.0 → urgent inspection
3. Trend kurtosis over time — rising trend indicates active fault
4. Check lubrication — contaminated grease causes kurtosis increase

### Elevated Temperature at Bearing

**Possible causes:**
1. Inadequate lubrication (most common)
2. Overloading
3. Over-greasing
4. Misalignment
5. Electrical discharge through bearing (EDM damage)

**Diagnostic steps:**
1. Check lubricant level and quality
2. Remove excess grease if over-greased
3. Check alignment
4. Verify current through bearing (insulated bearings may be needed for VFD-driven motors)

### Unusual Noise

**Squealing / High-pitched sound:**
- Insufficient lubrication
- Inner race damage
- Action: Add lubrication immediately; inspect bearing

**Rumbling / Low-frequency sound:**
- Outer race damage
- Contamination
- Action: Plan bearing inspection

**Clicking / Irregular sound:**
- Rolling element damage
- Cage damage
- Action: Immediate inspection

**Continuous metallic noise:**
- Advanced bearing failure
- Action: Stop equipment and inspect immediately

## Contamination Issues

### Water Contamination
- Causes: Seal failure, wash-down water ingress
- Signs: Milky grease, corrosion on bearing surfaces
- Action: Replace seals, flush bearing housing, re-grease
- Prevention: Use sealed bearings in wet environments

### Particulate Contamination
- Causes: Poor sealing, contaminated lubricant, nearby grinding/cutting
- Signs: Abrasive wear pattern, scored raceways
- Action: Improve sealing, filter lubrication system
- Prevention: Use appropriate seal type for environment

### Chemical Contamination
- Causes: Process fluid ingress, incorrect lubricant mixing
- Action: Clean thoroughly, identify contamination source, re-lubricate

## Installation Errors

### Common Installation Mistakes
1. **Incorrect mounting force** — always mount on the rotating ring
2. **Impact mounting** — never hammer directly on bearing
3. **Contamination during installation** — always work in clean conditions
4. **Wrong interference fit** — verify shaft/housing dimensions
5. **Misalignment during installation** — use alignment tools

### Signs of Installation Error
- Immediate vibration elevation after replacement
- Premature failure (within weeks of installation)
- Unusual temperature on startup
- Noise immediately after installation

## Electrical Bearing Damage (EDM)

### Cause
Variable frequency drives (VFDs) can generate bearing currents that discharge through bearings.

### Signs
- Frosting, pitting pattern on raceway (fluting)
- Gray discoloration of bearing surfaces
- Premature failure in VFD-driven motors

### Solutions
- Install insulated bearings on drive end
- Add shaft grounding brush
- Use ceramic-coated bearings
- Ensure proper motor grounding
